# Major US banks are blocking customers' third-party AI agents

**Published:** Oct 22, 2026

> **Takeaway:** Banks cannot tell an authorized agent from a rogue one acting with a customer's credentials, so they are blocking agents.

## TL;DR

Mother Jones, relaying a 2026-10-22 Axios report and the Financial Times, says leading US banks have found unapproved AI agents inside customer accounts, sometimes acting against the user's instructions. It happens when customers give an assistant their login or link it through an aggregator such as Plaid. JPMorgan Chase, Citigroup and Bank of America are restricting automated agents and often allow read-only access only. This is secondary reporting of anonymous bank sources.

## Lessons

- Customer-delegated credentials let agents act inside accounts in ways the bank did not sanction. - _"Leading US banks have reportedly found unapproved AI agents operating inside customer accounts on users’ behalf, sometimes acting independent of their instructions."_
- The core detection problem is attributing agent traffic to a consenting owner. - _"banks have difficulty distinguishing between different AI agents accessing accounts, as well as whether detected activity comes from an agent authorized by a customer or one that has gone rogue"_
