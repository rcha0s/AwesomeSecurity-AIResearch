# Citizen Lab: Candiru spyware reached Al Jazeera devices through targeted ads

**Published:** Oct 15, 2026

> **Takeaway:** Real-time ad bidding can deliver zero-click spyware; ad vendors cut off the abuser only after Citizen Lab disclosed it.

## TL;DR

Citizen Lab reports AdFection: Candiru spyware infected Al Jazeera devices through targeted advertisements, which it calls the first public technical confirmation of zero-click mercenary spyware delivered through ads. Discover-Tech, an Israeli firm that looks like a normal ad company, ran a platform that fingerprints devices and infects them when an ad renders. Four billion bidding records show its ads on almost 200,000 sites and apps. Google, IAB Europe, Beeswax, Vungle and Eskimi cut its access after disclosure.

## Lessons

- Ads delivered a zero-click mercenary spyware attack. - _"first public technical confirmation of a zero-click mercenary spyware attack delivered via targeted advertising, which we call AdFection"_
- Ad industry vendor lists failed to catch the abuse. - _"Discover-Tech has appeared on lists of “certified” and “approved” vendors maintained by Google and advertising industry association IAB Europe"_
- Scale: ads reached nearly 200,000 sites and apps in four months. - _"Analysis of four billion Discover-Tech bidding records shows that during a four-month period, the company’s ads were shown on almost 200,000 unique websites and Android/iOS apps in dozens of countries."_
- Disconnecting the vendor was possible after disclosure. - _"Google, IAB Europe, Beeswax, Vungle (Liftoff), and Eskimi took action to disconnect Discover-Tech’s access to the digital advertising ecosystem."_
